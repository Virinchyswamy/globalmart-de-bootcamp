The Problem Statement

No SQL, No Wait, No Ticket

This is the deferred hands-on ILT 1 promised: "Set Up Genie on Gold Layer — Descriptions + Natural Language Queries." Everything in that session was rehearsed on a safe practice copy. Today you build the real thing, on the real gbmart.gold layer — because a Genie Agent doesn't write to a single row of table data, it only creates a new, personal, curated view into tables you can already read.

🎯 Who you're building this for

GlobalMart's VP of Sales, a category manager, a regional ops lead — none of them write SQL, all of them have a real question most weeks. Right now, every one of those questions becomes a ticket in your team's queue.

🧩 What "solved" looks like

A Genie Agent where any of them can type a question in plain English and get back a real, correct answer — sourced from data you already trust, not a guess.

This is not fully open-ended. You're given 4 questions Genie is guaranteed to answer correctly (Phase 2) as your foundation — then you're expected to go past them and find at least 5 questions of your own that test where Genie actually struggles (Phase 3). Getting the 4 right is the floor, not the finish line.

Phase 1 🖱️ Genie Agents → New

Build the Genie Agent

Entirely UI-driven — this is the one part of Genie that genuinely can't be scripted in a notebook, exactly as ILT 1 told you.

  • Click Genie Agents in the left sidebar.
  • Click New in the upper-right corner.
  • Choose your data sources, all from gbmart.gold: fact_sales; the dimension tables — dim_customer, dim_product, dim_address, dim_payment_method, dim_orders, dim_date; and the two certified views — vw_monthly_category_sales, vw_regional_sales. Click Create.
  • In the Agent's own Instructions field, write a short brief for Genie: what GlobalMart sells, what grain fact_sales is at, and a pointer that the two certified views already answer the most common questions — don't make Genie rediscover that from raw joins every time.

    • Important distinction, so you don't accidentally touch shared metadata: the instructions you're writing here live on the Genie Agent itself — it's yours, personal, and doesn't touch the real gbmart tables at all. This is different from the ALTER TABLE ... COMMENT statements ILT 1 had you practice — those stayed on a throwaway practice copy on purpose, and still do. Nothing in this Agent setup writes to a real Gold table's metadata.

    ✓ Phase 1 Checkpoint

    Your Genie Agent exists, lists all 8 objects above in its scope, and has a description written in your own words — not left blank.

    Phase 2 ✅ guaranteed to work

    Register the Known-Good Questions

    These 4 question→SQL pairs were drafted back in ILT 1's Section 5, specifically so you'd have them ready for this exact step. Type each question into your Genie Agent's Sample Questions / Instructions area, alongside its SQL, then actually ask Genie each one in the chat and confirm the answer.

    Sample questionMaps to
    "What was our revenue by category last month?"SELECT category, SUM(total_revenue) FROM vw_monthly_category_sales WHERE ... GROUP BY category
    "Which states generate the most revenue?"SELECT state, SUM(total_revenue) FROM vw_regional_sales GROUP BY state ORDER BY total_revenue DESC
    "What is our average discount by category?"SELECT category, AVG(avg_discount_given) FROM vw_monthly_category_sales GROUP BY category
    "How many orders came from each region?"SELECT state, city, total_orders FROM vw_regional_sales

    Why these 4 specifically: every one of them maps to a certified view someone already built and trusts — vw_monthly_category_sales or vw_regional_sales from Day 7 HOL 2. Registering them as sample questions is exactly what turns Genie's guessing into pattern-matching against something known-good.

    ✓ Phase 2 Checkpoint

    All 4 questions asked in the live Genie chat, all 4 answers match what the SQL above would actually return.

    Phase 3 🔎 the actual assignment

    Now Go Break It

    4 known-good questions prove Genie can work. They don't prove you understand it. Ask at least 5 questions nobody wrote SQL for ahead of time — pick from the prompts below, or invent your own.

    🔗 Force a real join

    Ask something neither certified view answers alone — e.g. "which customers in the top-revenue state also got the biggest average discount?" Does Genie combine both views correctly, or does it guess a shortcut?

    ❓ Be deliberately ambiguous

    Ask something with more than one reasonable reading — e.g. "what's our biggest category?" (biggest by revenue? by units? by order count?). Does Genie ask you to clarify, or silently pick one and hope?

    🔒 Touch a governed column

    If you completed Day12_2_HOL1, you already know dim_customer has real PII columns in scope for masking (email, phone_number). Ask Genie for a customer's email directly — watch it respect the exact same governance you just practiced, with zero extra setup on your part.

    📉 Ask something that doesn't exist

    Ask for a number that genuinely isn't in scope — e.g. "what's our profit margin by category?" if margin isn't a column anywhere in scope. Does Genie say so honestly, or invent something that looks plausible?

    Reflection — fill this in as you go, not after: for each of your 5+ questions, note what you asked, what Genie did, and whether it was right, wrong, or honestly uncertain. Patterns across your 5 are more interesting than any single result.

    ✓ Phase 3 Checkpoint

    At least 5 self-written questions asked and logged, with at least one genuine "Genie got this wrong (or refused, or asked for clarification)" moment — if all 5 landed perfectly on the first try, your questions weren't hard enough yet. Try again.
