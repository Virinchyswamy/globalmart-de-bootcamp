Part 1 ⏱ ~20–25 min

All-Purpose Compute vs. Serverless — With Real Numbers This Time

What You Already Know (ILT 3 Recap)

Two things carry forward directly: DBU + cloud VM cost are two separate meters, billed by the minute, whether or not a cluster is doing useful work — and Serverless compute (SQL warehouses, serverless jobs) removes cluster startup/idle time from the equation entirely, because you pay for query execution, not for a cluster sitting around waiting. ILT 3 stated both facts but never ran the numbers side by side. That's today.

The Direct Comparison

All-Purpose ComputeServerless
Billing unitDBU rate + separate cloud VM cost, per nodeOne blended usage-based rate — no separate VM line item
Idle-time costFull cost continues until autotermination_minutes kicks inEffectively none — billed for active execution only
Startup latencyCluster must spin up before the first command runsNear-instant — no cluster provisioning wait
Node sizingYou choose node type/count — right-sizing is your jobAbstracted away — Databricks scales it for you
Best-fit workloadInteractive development, exploration, teaching sessionsBursty, unpredictable, ad-hoc workloads — BI dashboards, occasional queries
Autotermination riskYou set it yourself; 0 means "never" — a real riskNot applicable the same way — no persistent idle cluster to forget about

Worksheet: Run the Numbers on Two Real Scenarios

Same illustrative rates ILT 3 already used — ILLUSTRATIVE_DBU_RATE_USD = 0.55, ILLUSTRATIVE_VM_RATE_USD_PER_HR = 0.90, 4-node cluster → hourly_cost(4) = $8.00/hour for All-Purpose. Serverless gets one new illustrative blended rate: $12.00/hour of actual query execution — higher per hour (the convenience premium is real), but you're only ever billed for the hours actually spent running something.

Scenario A — The BI Team's Ad-Hoc Queries

An All-Purpose cluster is kept running 8 hours/day "just in case," business days only (22 days/month) — but the team's actual query execution across the whole day totals only about 45 minutes (0.75 hours).

All-Purpose, kept on 8h/day × 22 days$8.00 × 8 × 22 = $1,408 / month
Serverless, billed only for the 0.75h actually used × 22 days$12.00 × 0.75 × 22 = $198 / month
Serverless saves$1,210 / month (~86% less)

Scenario B — The Nightly Batch Run

Reusing GlobalMart's own real figure from ILT 3: the nightly Bronze → Silver → Gold run takes 1.5 hours of actual processing. Someone left an All-Purpose cluster running 24/7 instead of using a Job Cluster.

All-Purpose, left on 24/7 × 30 days (ILT 3's own number)$8.00 × 24 × 30 = $5,760 / month
Serverless, billed for 1.5h actual processing × 30 days$12.00 × 1.5 × 30 = $540 / month
Serverless saves vs. All-Purpose left on$5,220 / month (~91% less)

Honest callout, don't skip this: for Scenario B specifically, ILT 3's own Job Cluster answer — $360/month — still beats Serverless's $540/month. For a workload with a fixed, predictable duration and a real schedule, a Job Cluster wins outright. Serverless's real edge is Scenario A: work that's bursty, unpredictable, or genuinely ad-hoc, where you can't cleanly schedule a job cluster around it in the first place. All-Purpose left running is the wrong answer in both scenarios — the interesting choice is Job Cluster vs. Serverless, not All-Purpose vs. either of them.

✓ Debrief

Ask the room: why did Serverless demolish All-Purpose in Scenario A but only "win" against a mistake (All-Purpose left on) in Scenario B? The answer is always idle time — Serverless has none, structurally. Close by pointing at system.billing.usage and system.compute.clusters (from ILT 3, Part 1) as where you'd actually pull real numbers like these on a client's live workspace.

Prep Time — 30 Minutes, Before Any Round Starts

  • Post or read out the group table above so everyone knows their group, their pair, and both their topics up front.
  • Prepping as interviewer: skim your interviewer-round topic's question bank below and pick the 2-3 questions you want to lead with — you don't need all 6, just enough for a real 8-minute conversation with follow-up.
  • Prepping as interviewee: for your other topic, think through how you'd actually answer — not a script, just enough that you're not starting from zero when the round begins.
  • Both kinds of prep happen in the same 30-minute block, since everyone already knows both their topics and both their roles going in.

    • Question Bank — By Topic

    Interviewers: pick 2–3 from your topic's list, or invent your own in the same spirit. 8 minutes is enough for 2–3 questions with real follow-up, not a checklist.

    1

    Medallion Architecture & Data Modeling

    Design decisions — the ones that actually separate a job-ready DE from someone who's only followed a tutorial
    • Walk me through how you'd design the Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers for a completely new data domain you've never seen before.
    • When do you use SCD Type 1 versus SCD Type 2 for a dimension table? Give a real example of each.
    • What's the difference between a surrogate key and a natural key, and why do fact tables usually join dimensions on the surrogate key?
    • A teammate wants to build a dimension table straight from a transactional line-item table. Why would you push back?
    • How do you decide the grain of a fact table before writing a single line of code?
    • What makes a Kimball star schema the right — or wrong — choice for a given project? What do you actually ask the business before deciding?
    2

    Data Quality & Pipeline Reliability

    The judgment calls that show up in almost every real DE role
    • Walk me through your process the moment a DQ scan flags something unexpected — what do you check before deciding fix, flag, or quarantine?
    • Give an example of data that "looks wrong" but is actually a valid business signal, not a defect.
    • What makes an ETL pipeline idempotent, and why does that matter when a job gets re-run after a failure?
    • Does a null value in a business-critical column always mean bad data? How do you tell the difference?
    • What's the real risk of quarantining a row versus flagging it and keeping it — how do you decide which applies?
    • A pipeline has been silently dropping rows for 3 weeks. How would you even discover that, and how do you stop it happening again?
    3

    Incremental Processing & CDC

    The mechanics behind almost every production pipeline you'll actually touch
    • Explain Change Data Feed in plain language to someone who's only ever done full-table reloads.
    • What's the real difference between Autoloader's addNewColumns and failOnNewColumns, and when would you pick each?
    • Walk me through what a MERGE statement actually does, step by step, for a new row versus an updated row.
    • Your CDF read returns two rows for the same key in one batch. What causes that, and how do you fix it before it breaks your MERGE?
    • How do you know your Autoloader checkpoint is safe, and what happens if someone deletes it by accident?
    • What's the real-world difference between SCD1 and SCD2 in terms of what a business team can actually ask afterward?
    4

    Orchestration & Production Operations

    What separates "it works on my cluster" from a real production pipeline
    • Walk me through how you'd design a Workflow DAG for a pipeline with 3 Bronze, 3 Silver, and 1 Gold notebook with real dependencies between them.
    • What's the difference between a Job Cluster and an All-Purpose Cluster, and why does that choice matter for a scheduled production pipeline?
    • A production Job fails at 2 AM. Walk me through your actual triage process, in order.
    • What's a Dead Letter Queue, and why would a production pipeline need one instead of just letting bad records fail the whole job?
    • How do you pass environment configuration — catalog, schema — into a Job without hardcoding anything into the notebook?
    • What's the real risk of running Bronze, Silver, and Gold notebooks manually every day instead of orchestrating them?
    5

    Cost, Performance & Governance

    Straight out of Part 1 — the vocabulary has to actually show up here
    • When would you recommend Serverless over a Job Cluster, and when would you recommend the opposite?
    • What's the single biggest lever for reducing Databricks compute cost, and why does it matter more than almost anything else?
    • Explain the difference between a row filter and a column mask in Unity Catalog, with a real business scenario for each.
    • Why does compute usually dominate the bill over storage, and how would you explain that to a client who assumes storage is the expensive part?
    • What's the actual risk of leaving autotermination_minutes at 0 on a shared interactive cluster?
    • A client asks whether their PII data is safe. What do you actually check before answering that honestly?
    ✓ Closing the Room

    No formal scoring today — this is a confidence-building send-off, not a graded assessment. After Round 2 (and the trio's Round C), invite 1–2 groups to demo a round live for everyone — pick ones covering different topics for variety. There's slack in the room until 6 PM, so let the demo run long if people are enjoying it, and leave time after for open Q&A.
