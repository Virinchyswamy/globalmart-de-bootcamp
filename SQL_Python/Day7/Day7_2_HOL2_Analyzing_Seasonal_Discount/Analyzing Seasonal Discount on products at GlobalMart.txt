# Copy of Analyzing Seasonal Discount on products at GlobalMart
## Content Type
Scenario

## Overview
The pricing team at GlobalMart aims to analyze the impact of seasonal discounts on their product prices. They plan to use two list comprehensions to achieve this:

* Apply Discounts: Adjust the original prices of products by applying seasonal discounts.
* Categorize Products: Classify products based on their discounted prices.

GlobalMart conducts various seasonal discount campaigns to attract customers and boost sales. By analyzing the impact of these discounts on different product categories, the team can better plan future promotions and pricing strategies.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand and apply data transformation techniques to facilitate easier analysis
- Apply list comprehension to process the data

## Prerequisites
- Familiarity with Pandas library for data manipulation and analysis.
- Understanding of lambda functions and their application in data analysis.
