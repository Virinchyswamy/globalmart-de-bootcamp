Shape — the Real, Running GlobalMart Fact Table

fact_sales
├── fact_sales_sk        (surrogate PK -- the ONLY generated key)
├── Payment_ID, Customer_ID, Product_ID, Order_ID, Address_ID, Time_ID  (natural keys)
├── Quantity_purchased, Actual_price, Discounted_price  (measures)
└── Sales_amount           (measure = Quantity_purchased × Discounted_price)
From ILT 1: every FK here is a natural/business key, not a surrogate key — a deliberate, documented simplification. dim_customer/dim_product still have proper SCD2 surrogate keys; this fact table just isn't wired to them that way.

Step 9 — Serve It: Business-Facing Gold Views

Nobody outside the data team queries fact_sales directly day to day. Real recurring questions get answered by views — one agreed-upon definition everyone reports against, instead of every analyst writing a slightly different join.

ViewWho runs it dailyQuestion it answers
vw_monthly_category_salesCategory / merchandising managersHow is each category trending month-over-month, and who's leaning on discounts?
vw_regional_salesRegional / logistics ops leadsWhich states/cities drive the most revenue, and what's the average order size?
Why a VIEW, not a materialized table? At fact_sales's current size, a view recomputes fresh on every query — no refresh job to schedule, always reflects current data. Promote to a materialized table only once a dashboard actually gets slow at much larger scale.

Key Takeaways

  • Verify the row count after every join — this is where fan-outs hide
  • is_current = true filters are not optional on SCD2 dimension joins
  • Natural-key joins were a deliberate, understood tradeoff — not a shortcut
  • The two views ARE the deliverable for most downstream consumers
